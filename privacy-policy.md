# Privacy Policy — LEKO

**Last updated: 24 August 2026**

LEKO is a personal finance app that lets you record accounts,
transactions, recurring bills and income, and see summaries of your
spending. This policy explains what it stores and why.

It is written to describe exactly what the app does — not to cover
things it might do one day.

## What is collected

**Your email address.** Used only to create your account and sign you
in. It is not used for marketing, and there is no mailing list.

**The financial information you enter.** Account names and balances,
transactions, merchants, categories, notes, recurring rules and
paycheck records. All of it is typed in by you; the app does not
gather it from anywhere else.

**Nothing else.** There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking,
no location collection, no device fingerprinting, and no third-party
SDK watching how you use the app.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored in a hosted Postgres database provided by Supabase, and
protected by row-level security rules so that your account can only
ever read and write its own rows.

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is not used to
train anything. Nobody is sent reports about you.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Delete my account** permanently deletes your account and
everything stored against it: accounts, transactions, categories,
recurring rules and settings. This happens immediately and cannot be
undone. You do not need to email anyone to request it.

Signing out, by contrast, leaves your data intact for the next sign-in.

## Security

Access requires your email and password. The app also offers an
optional app lock (Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode) that
protects the app on your device.

No system is perfectly secure, and this app is built and maintained by
one person rather than a company with a security team. Please keep that
in mind when deciding what to record in it.

## Children

LEKO is not directed at children under 13, and accounts should
not be created for them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material
changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
